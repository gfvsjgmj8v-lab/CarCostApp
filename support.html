Frequently asked questions

Are the cost calculations exact?

Results are estimates based on entered values and assumptions. Actual costs can vary by vehicle, location, driving style and prices.

How do I report incorrect vehicle data?

Email us with the vehicle make, model, year and the detail that needs correction.
